Waste Management Methods

  • Recycling - Converting waste into reusable materials.
  • Composting - Turning organic waste into fertilizer.
  • Incineration - Burning waste to generate energy (with emissions control).
  • Landfilling - Safe containment of waste; last resort.
  • Waste Minimization - Reducing waste generation through conscious choices.
  • Biogas Production - Fermentation of organic waste for energy.
