Health & Environmental Effects

  • Health Risks: Improper waste handling can cause infections, respiratory issues, water/soil contamination, and spread diseases.
  • Environmental Impact: Pollution (air, water, soil), greenhouse gas emissions, loss of biodiversity, and resource depletion.
  • Proper waste management protects public health and preserves the environment.
