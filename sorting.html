How to Sort Waste

  1. Separate organic waste from other types (compost bin).
  2. Clean and sort recyclables into correct bins (glass, paper, metal, plastic).
  3. Dispose hazardous and electronic waste at special collection points.
  4. Medical waste should go to dedicated containers/bins.
  5. General waste goes into the residual bin.
